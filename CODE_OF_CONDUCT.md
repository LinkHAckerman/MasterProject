# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Magnum Opus is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part in it to be able to do so respectfully and without harassment. This Code of Conduct sets out the behavior we expect and how problems are handled. It applies to everyone, including the Maintainer.

## Where this applies

This Code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, commit messages, and any other channel run by or on behalf of the project. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people working in languages or tools unfamiliar to them.
- Credit others for their work and ideas.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments
- Discrimination or hostile conduct based on age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic
- Threats or encouragement of violence or self-harm
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or spam, including repeated off-topic or promotional posts
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as an address or personal contact details) without their explicit permission
- Submitting malicious code, or attempting to scam others (for example with fraudulent tokens, phishing links, or "investment" offers)
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Crypto-specific conduct

This is a crypto and Web3 project, which attracts scams. Do not use project spaces to promote tokens, airdrops, giveaways, trading signals, or investment schemes. Do not ask others for private keys, seed phrases, or wallet access. Such posts will be removed and the author may be blocked.

## Automated contributors

Parts of this repository are written and committed by automated AI agents. Automation does not change the standards for what is in the project: content produced by it is held to the same expectations as any other contribution, and the Maintainer is responsible for the project's content. If something generated in this repository is offensive, infringing, or harmful, please report it.

## Enforcement

The Maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair and appropriate action in response to behavior they consider a violation. Depending on the situation, that can include:

1. A private or public reminder of the expectations
2. A warning
3. Removal, editing, or locking of comments, issues, or pull requests
4. A temporary ban from project spaces
5. A permanent ban, and reporting the conduct to GitHub

The Maintainer may remove or reject any content that does not follow this Code, and will try to explain their decision. Decisions are made in good faith, at the Maintainer's discretion.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code, please report it privately:

- Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/LinkHAckerman/MasterProject/security) form and mark the report as a conduct issue, or
- Open an issue asking for a private channel, **without** including details of the incident, and the Maintainer will contact you, or
- Report content directly to GitHub using its **Report content** feature.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. The Maintainer will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reports an incident. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation of this Code.

If you are reporting behavior by the Maintainer, please report it to GitHub through **Report content**.
